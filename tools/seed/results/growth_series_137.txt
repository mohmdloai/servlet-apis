VERDICT: the seeder (and the V77 hand-backfill) stamped each milestone in a single burst —
every milestone spans at most ~3 days and lands in exactly ONE week bucket and ONE month
bucket. A series over perfdb is one bucket tall per stage and proves nothing about bucket
ordering, sparsity, or window edges. Exactly the case the story anticipated: the ITs
(PlatformGrowthIT) build their own scratch fixtures with events spread across buckets by
direct org_milestone inserts, and never depend on perfdb. perfdb here answers only the plan
and latency question, which it does answer — the table's size class, not its time spread, is
what decides the plan.

EXPLAIN (ANALYZE, BUFFERS) — warm-twice per bucket × bounded/unbounded window, path=all
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Query shape (the repository's own, jOOQ-generated equivalent):

  SELECT om.milestone, date_trunc($bucket, om.reached_at, 'UTC') AS period, count(*)
  FROM org JOIN org_milestone om ON om.org_id = org.id
  [WHERE om.reached_at >= now() - interval '90 days']
  GROUP BY om.milestone, period ORDER BY om.milestone, period;

Plan, identical in all four shapes: Seq Scan(org_milestone) + Seq Scan(org) → Hash Join →
HashAggregate → quicksort. No index considered, none exists to consider beyond the PK —
V77's header records the planner refusing every candidate on this table; not re-litigated.

  shape                     run1 exec   run2 exec   (planning 1.1–3.7 ms both runs)
  week  × all   (1003 rows)  1.39 ms     2.23 ms
  week  × 90d   ( 403 rows)  0.75 ms     0.68 ms
  month × all   (1003 rows)  0.98 ms     0.87 ms
  month × 90d   ( 403 rows)  0.70 ms     0.71 ms

Range: 0.68–2.23 ms warm across all shapes — comfortably inside the story's ~10 ms bar; the
expected "trivial Seq Scan + HashAggregate, single-digit ms" is confirmed by measurement, not
assumed. (The 403-row window shapes exist because ~400 of the 1003 rows are the 2026-07-26
burst — see spread table.)
